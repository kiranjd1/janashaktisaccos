Welcome to Janashakti Saving and Credit Cooperative

Established in 2014 A.D. (B.S. 2071), Janashakti Saving and Credit Cooperative is dedicated to providing its members the best possible services with the vision of a beautiful society with secure financial self-reliance and prosperity. Our main goal is to uplift the financial backgrounds of our members and their communities by initiating healthy saving habits and utilizing our credit facilities in various productive sectors.

Overview

Founded in 2014 with 31 members, Janashakti SACCOS has grown to serve over 5,000 members across six local bodies in Jhapa. Rooted in community and trust, we provide savings and credit services that empower individuals, strengthen families, and promote sustainable financial security.
