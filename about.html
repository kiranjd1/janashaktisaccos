Our Introduction

Founded in 2014 A.D. with 31 dedicated members, Janashakti Saving and Credit Co-operative Ltd. (Janashakti SACCOS) has grown into a trusted financial institution with more than 5,000 members across six local bodies of Jhapa district: Birtamod, Haldibari, Kankai, Barhadashi, Arjundhara, and Bhadrapur. Established with the vision of building a prosperous society rooted in financial self-reliance, Janashakti is committed to uplifting the economic backgrounds of its members and their communities. By encouraging healthy saving habits and providing accessible credit facilities in diverse productive sectors, the cooperative empowers individuals, strengthens families, and fosters collective prosperity. With a steadfast dedication to community, trust, and sustainable growth, Janashakti continues to serve as a reliable platform for members seeking stability, opportunity, and a brighter future.
