Our Loan Services

Business Loan

Collateral / Guarantor based loan to utilize in business start-ups and growth.

Bachat Barabar Loan

Loan against the security of member's own saving balance.

Swarojgar Loan

Loan services to empower youths in self-employment opportunities in collaboration with Youth and Small Entrepreneur Self Employment Fund.

Krishi Loan

Loan facility for members engaged in agricultural sectors.

Mahila Uddyami Loan

Loan facility to be utilized by our female members in entrepreneurship and businesses.

Karmachari Loan

Personal Loan for the employees of our organization.

Overview

Founded in 2014 with 31 members, Janashakti SACCOS has grown to serve over 5,000 members across six local bodies in Jhapa. Rooted in community and trust, we provide savings and credit services that empower individuals, strengthen families, and promote sustainable financial security.
